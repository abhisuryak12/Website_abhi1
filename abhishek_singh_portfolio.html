About Me

Who I Am

I am working as an Assistant Professor (Statistics) and Faculty Placement Coordinator at the Department of Mathematics and Statistics, Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT-WPU, Kothrud, Pune, Maharashtra, India since December 2022.

My research is focused on estimating finite population parameters using auxiliary characteristics under Ranked Set Sampling — a cost-efficient, time-saving alternative to simple random sampling. I work across neutrosophic environments, measurement error scenarios, and sensitive variable estimation.

I am an approved PhD supervisor in both Mathematics and Statistics, have 16+ published papers, and provide research internships to UG/PG students. I was promoted to Assistant Professor Level 11 in July 2025.

Research

Research Areas & Interests

📐
Sampling Theory & Ranked Set Sampling
Finite population parameter estimation using auxiliary characteristics. Ranked set sampling as a cost-efficient substitute for simple random sampling, with applications in solar energy and COVID-19 data.
🌐
Neutrosophic Statistics
Pioneered the Neutrosophic Ranked Set Sampling method and generalized estimators for indeterminate/interval type data. Introduced neutrosophic stratified sampling, opening new research frontiers.
🔬
Statistical Inference & Bayesian Methods
Bayesian inference, reliability, survival analysis, biostatistics, control charts, and measurement error modeling under correlated and uncorrelated scenarios.
🤖
Machine Learning with Statistics
Integration of statistical concepts with AI/ML techniques. Forecasting, time series analysis, and neutrosophic statistics combined with machine learning methods.
